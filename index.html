🍦 The Ice Cream Date Challenge

1
The Goal: Zariah and Serenity work together as a team. When their combined chore ratings hit 80% Excellent Job! in at least 4 out of any 5 consecutive weeks, they earn an ice cream date with one or both parents.
2
How the score is calculated: All chores from both girls in a week are counted together as one combined total. The team score is never split by individual girl.
3
The grace week: The girls get one grace week per 5-week window. If they hit 80% in 4 of 5 weeks, they still earn the reward.
4
When it resets: Once earned, the 5-week window resets and they start fresh. Each earned date is logged in the app.
5
The reward happens fast: The ice cream date takes place within the same week it's earned.

🤝 Teamwork Rules

1
No blame, ever. The team score is shared equally — no pointing fingers. We cheer each other on and try again.
2
Cheer each other on. Zariah and Serenity are on the same side. Encouraging your sister counts just as much as doing your own chores well.
3
Individual scores are private. Mom and Dad track individual performance separately — the girls only see the team score together.

⭐ Weekly Reward Rules

1
Maximum 2 rewards per girl per week. The limit resets every Monday.
2
Rewards are earned, not guaranteed. Granted by Mom or Dad based on chore performance.
3
Individual rewards are separate from the ice cream date challenge.

📋 Chore Rules

1
Excellent Job! means the chore was completed fully, without being asked twice, and done with care.
2
Needs Work means the chore was incomplete, done carelessly, or required significant reminders.
3
Zariah's daily limit: No more than 3 chores per day.
4
Serenity's daily limit: No more than 4 chores per day.
